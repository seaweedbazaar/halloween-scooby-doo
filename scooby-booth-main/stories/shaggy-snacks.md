# Shaggy: The Taffy Monster

CHARACTERS:
- Shaggy: the classic Scooby-Doo character: a tall, lanky, slouching young man with shaggy light brown hair, a scruffy little chin goatee, a baggy olive-green V-neck t-shirt, maroon-brown bell-bottom pants and brown shoes. Always hungry. Same look in every panel. Appears only once per panel.
- The Taffy Monster: ONE single person, a blobby, dripping monster made of stretchy bright pink taffy, with glowing yellow eyes and long gooey arms. Secretly the grumpy old owner of the candy factory in a pink rubber monster costume. Underneath, he is a completely normal HUMAN: a short, round old man with bushy white eyebrows, a white walrus mustache and a red bow tie. There is only one monster in the whole comic, and it does NOT appear in panel 1.

STORY:
Panel 1: Night outside an old, closed candy factory, in deep blue and purple, with a big round moon. Shaggy presses his face against the factory's front window, drooling, staring at the giant jars of candy inside. The hero stands next to him, holding a flashlight, giving him a skeptical look. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to Shaggy's mouth: "LIKE, JUST ONE TINY SNACK?"
Panel 2: Inside the candy factory in warm flat orange, surrounded by big glass candy jars and a huge vat of bubbling pink taffy. Exactly three figures, left to right: the Taffy Monster, the hero, and Shaggy. On the left, behind them, the Taffy Monster's head and glowing yellow eyes rise up out of the vat. In the middle, the hero has spotted it: frozen in horror, hair standing on end, eyes huge, mouth wide open, flashlight shaking, frantically pointing back at the vat with their other hand. On the right, Shaggy, right next to the hero, has noticed nothing: eyes closed in bliss, happily stuffing his cheeks and pockets with candy. Shaggy appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "BLORP!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 3: The factory floor in flat lime green. Exactly three figures, left to right: the Taffy Monster, the hero, and Shaggy. On the left, the Taffy Monster is stuck to the floor, buried up to its chest under a huge glob of pink taffy that has just poured down on it from a chute in the ceiling, its gooey arms flailing. In the middle, the hero stands with one hand still gripping a big lever on the wall, looking calm and pleased. On the right, Shaggy peeks out from behind the hero, candy spilling from his pockets. Each figure appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "SPLAT!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 4: The factory in deep blue. Exactly three people, left to right: the hero, Shaggy, and the culprit. On the left, the hero and Shaggy high-five; Shaggy's cheeks are still stuffed with candy. On the right, apart from them, the unmasked culprit sits in a big puddle of pink taffy, stuck to the floor, arms crossed and scowling: the short, round old man with bushy white eyebrows, a white walrus mustache and a red bow tie. Only ONE pink monster mask lies limp and empty in the taffy beside him. There is no costume body, no second mask and no second villain. Shaggy appears only once in this panel. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing clearly to Shaggy's mouth: "LIKE, SWEETEST CASE EVER!"

TEXT: Exactly 2 speech bubbles (panels 1 and 4) and 2 sound effects (panels 2 and 3). Keep every line exactly as written; they are short on purpose. No writing anywhere in the art: the candy jars have no labels, and there are no words on the factory, the lever or any signs.
