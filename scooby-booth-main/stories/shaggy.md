# Shaggy: The S'more Werewolf

CHARACTERS:
- Shaggy: the classic Scooby-Doo character: a tall, lanky, slouching young man with shaggy light brown hair, a scruffy little chin goatee, a baggy olive-green V-neck t-shirt, maroon-brown bell-bottom pants and brown shoes. Jittery, easily scared, always hungry. Same look in every panel. Appears only once per panel.
- The Werewolf: ONE single person, a hunched, hairy werewolf with shaggy dark gray fur, pointed ears, sharp teeth and glowing yellow eyes. Secretly a grumpy old park ranger in a costume: a rubber werewolf mask, furry gloves and a furry suit. Underneath the mask he is a completely normal HUMAN: a bald old man with pink human skin and a gray mustache. No fur, no wolf ears, no wolf features on his real face or head.

STORY:
Panel 1: A forest campsite at night by a lake, in deep blue and purple, with a small campfire and a wooden dock stretching out over the water. The hero and Shaggy sit on a log by the fire, (only the two of them), roasting marshmallows on sticks. Shaggy holds a flashlight under his chin to look spooky while telling a scary story; the hero listens, smiling. In the dark bushes behind them, two glowing yellow eyes are watching, and they haven't noticed. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to Shaggy's mouth: "LIKE, THEY SAY THE WEREWOLF ONLY COMES OUT FOR S'MORES..."
Panel 2: Flat purple background with jagged action lines. Exactly three figures, left to right: the Werewolf, Shaggy, and the hero. On the left, the Werewolf bursts out of the bushes, arms raised, teeth bared. On the right, Shaggy has leapt straight into the hero's arms in terror: the hero holds him up like a scared puppy, Shaggy's long legs dangling, his hair standing on end, sweat drops flying. The hero looks surprised but stands firm. Shaggy appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "ZOINKS!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 3: The end of the wooden dock, with the lake in flat teal and blue. Exactly three figures, left to right: Shaggy, the hero, and the Werewolf. In the middle, the hero stands confidently at the edge of the dock, holding a giant s'more out over the water on a long stick, with Shaggy peeking out from behind the hero on the left. On the right, the Werewolf lunged for the s'more, missed, and fell off the dock into the shallow water, where he now sits soaking wet, still in his furry suit from the neck down. He has just pulled off his mask: it hangs limp and floppy from one hand, clearly a hollow, empty rubber mask. His real face is revealed: a grumpy bald old man with plain human skin and a gray mustache, with no fur on his face or head. He appears exactly once, with exactly one head. Shaggy appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "SPLASH!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 4: The campsite under a flat dark blue sky full of simple white stars, the campfire glowing orange in the background. Exactly three people, left to right: the ranger, the hero, and Shaggy. In the background on the left, the soggy park ranger sits grumpily on a stump, now in his ranger uniform and hat, with a towel around his neck. In the foreground in the middle and on the right, the hero and Shaggy high-five. Shaggy's other hand holds a stick with five marshmallows stacked on it, his cheeks stuffed and bulging. The hero laughs. Shaggy appears only once in this panel. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing clearly to Shaggy's mouth: "LIKE, ALL THAT BRAVERY MADE ME HUNGRY!"

TEXT: Exactly 2 speech bubbles (panels 1 and 4) and 2 sound effects (panels 2 and 3). No writing on the dock, signs, or the marshmallow bag.
