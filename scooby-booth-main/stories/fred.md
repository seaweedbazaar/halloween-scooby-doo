# Fred: The Phantom of the Disco

CHARACTERS:
- Fred: the classic Scooby-Doo character: a slim young man about the same height and build as the hero, with neat blond hair swept to one side, a white long-sleeve shirt with a blue collar, an orange ascot, blue bell-bottom pants and brown shoes. Charming, confident, a little goofy. Same look in every panel. Appears only once per panel.
- The Phantom of the Disco: ONE single person, a tall glowing pale green ghost in a white 1970s disco suit with huge bell-bottoms and a wide pointed collar, a long black cape, and a blank white ghost mask with hollow black eyes. Secretly the grumpy old gym teacher, a stocky bald man with a bushy gray mustache and a whistle on a cord around his neck, who hates school dances.

STORY:
Panel 1: A 1970s school gym decorated for a dance, with purple and magenta lights on the walls, a mirrored disco ball hanging from the ceiling, paper streamers, and a light-up checkered dance floor. A few classmates dance in the background. The hero and Fred stand facing each other in the middle ground, both shown from the knees up at the same size. Fred offers his hand to the hero with a charming, slightly nervous grin. The hero smiles back. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to Fred's mouth: "THEY SAY THIS DANCE IS HAUNTED... WANT TO DANCE ANYWAY?"
Panel 2: The same gym, the walls and floor now in flat lime green. Exactly three figures, left to right: the Phantom of the Disco, the hero, and Fred. On the left, the Phantom of the Disco swoops onto the dance floor, cape spread wide, arms raised. The classmates run away screaming in the background. In the middle and on the right, the hero and Fred stand their ground side by side, startled but not running. Fred appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "WHOOOOO!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 3: The dance floor with flat orange and yellow spotlight beams in the background. Exactly three people, left to right: the hero, Fred, and the Phantom. On the left, the hero strikes a 1970s disco pose, one arm pointing up to the sky, one hand on the hip. In the middle, Fred (blond hair, white shirt, orange ascot, blue bell-bottoms) strikes the matching disco pose right beside the hero. They are two clearly different people: only ONE hero and ONE Fred in this panel. On the right, next to them, the Phantom tried to copy the move but has tripped over his own long cape and fallen flat on the dance floor. He sits up holding his white ghost mask in one hand, having just pulled it off, revealing the grumpy bald gym teacher with a gray mustache and a whistle around his neck. He appears exactly once, with exactly one head. Fred appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "THUD!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 4: The gym walls in deep blue, the disco ball overhead scattering small white sparkles across the background. The hero and Fred slow dance together in the middle of the empty dance floor, close, smiling at each other, a few small pink hearts floating above them. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing clearly to Fred's mouth: "SAVE ME EVERY DANCE?"

TEXT: Exactly 2 speech bubbles (panels 1 and 4) and 2 sound effects (panels 2 and 3). No writing on the walls or the floor.
