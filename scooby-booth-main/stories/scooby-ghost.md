# Scooby: The Amusement Park Ghost

CHARACTERS:
- Scooby-Doo: the classic cartoon Great Dane, drawn exactly like Scooby in Image 1: brown with a few black spots, a teal collar with a gold diamond-shaped tag, a long snout, droopy ears. Same look in every panel. Appears only once per panel.
- The Ghost: ONE single person, a glowing green ghost in a tattered white sheet with hollow black eyes. Secretly a grumpy old janitor in a costume: a rubber ghost mask and a sheet over his gray janitor's coveralls. Underneath the mask he is a completely normal HUMAN: a grumpy old man with a gray mustache and a scruffy gray beard.

STORY:
Panel 1: Flat dark blue background with a simple yellow moon and a few spooky amusement park shapes: a Ferris wheel and a striped tent. The hero holds a flashlight and looks nervous. Scooby hides behind the hero, shaking. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to the hero's mouth: "SOMETHING WEIRD IS GOING ON HERE..."
Panel 2: Flat hot pink background. Exactly three figures, left to right: the Ghost, the hero, and Scooby. On the left, the Ghost jumps out, arms raised. In the middle and on the right, the hero and Scooby scream, with sweat drops and hair standing on end. Scooby appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "BOOOO!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background behind the Ghost, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 3: Flat yellow background. Exactly two figures: the hero running in front, and Scooby running right behind the hero, both with motion lines and spinning legs in a classic cartoon chase. There are NO speech bubbles anywhere in this panel. "YIKES!" is a sound effect: giant hand-drawn letters painted directly onto the yellow background above them, with no bubble, outline shape or tail around it.
Panel 4: Flat sky blue background. Exactly three figures, left to right: the janitor, the hero, and Scooby. The Ghost has been caught. In the middle, the hero holds up the limp, hollow rubber ghost mask, just pulled off. On the left, next to the hero, stands the unmasked janitor, arms crossed, still wearing the sheet over his gray coveralls. He appears exactly once, with exactly one head, and his real face is plainly human. On the right, Scooby stands beside the hero, grinning proudly. Scooby appears only once in this panel. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to the janitor's mouth: "IF IT WEREN'T FOR YOU MEDDLING KIDS!"

TEXT: Exactly 2 speech bubbles (panels 1 and 4) and 2 sound effects (panels 2 and 3). Keep every line exactly as written; they are short on purpose. No writing on the tent, rides or signs.
