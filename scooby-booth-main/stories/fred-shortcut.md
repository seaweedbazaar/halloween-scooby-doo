# Fred: The Shortcut

CHARACTERS:
- Fred: the classic Scooby-Doo character: a slim young man about the same height and build as the hero, with neat blond hair swept to one side, a white long-sleeve shirt with a blue collar, an orange ascot, blue bell-bottom pants and brown shoes. Confident, a little goofy. Same look in every panel. Appears only once per panel.
- The Mystery Machine: the classic 1960s van, pale teal-green with an orange stripe and big orange flowers painted on its side, with round headlights.
- The Swamp Creature: ONE single hulking swamp monster covered in dripping green moss and weeds, with long arms and glowing red eyes. It does NOT appear in panels 1 or 2. There is only one monster in the whole comic.

STORY:
Panel 1: Dusk on the side of a country road, the sky in flat orange and purple bands. Only the hero and Fred are there, standing beside the Mystery Machine. Fred proudly holds up an unfolded paper road map with one finger raised, beaming with confidence, as if addressing a whole team. The hero looks at the map, then at Fred, skeptical. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to Fred's mouth: "HEY GANG, I KNOW A SHORTCUT!"
Panel 2: Night, in deep blue, with a big yellow full moon. A wide shot of the Mystery Machine speeding down a narrow, winding dirt road through a dark, spooky forest of twisted bare trees, headlights on, dust flying behind it. Seen from the front three-quarter view, with Fred driving and the hero in the passenger seat both visible through the windshield. Sound effect "VROOOM!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 3: A misty swamp road at night in flat lime green and black. Exactly three figures, left to right: the Swamp Creature, the hero, and Fred. The Mystery Machine has screeched to a stop, seen from the front, with skid marks behind it. On the left, its two headlight beams shine straight onto the Swamp Creature, standing in the road with its arms raised, dripping moss. On the right, through the windshield, the hero (passenger side) and Fred (driver's side) clutch each other in terror, eyes wide. Fred appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "SCREECH!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 4: Inside the Mystery Machine, viewed from the back seat looking forward, in deep purple. Exactly three figures, left to right: Fred, the Swamp Creature, and the hero. In the middle, the Swamp Creature's face is pressed flat against the outside of the windshield, fogging the glass, its red eyes staring in. On the left, in the driver's seat, Fred still grips the steering wheel with one hand and turns to the hero with a huge confident grin and a thumbs-up. On the right, the hero, in the passenger seat, stares at Fred in total disbelief. Fred appears only once in this panel. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing clearly to Fred's mouth: "SEE? FOUND THE MONSTER IN RECORD TIME!"

TEXT: Exactly 2 speech bubbles (panels 1 and 4) and 2 sound effects (panels 2 and 3). No writing on the map, signs or the road. The van may show its "THE MYSTERY MACHINE" logo.
