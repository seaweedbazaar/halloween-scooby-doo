# Velma: The Museum Mummy

CHARACTERS:
- Velma: the classic Scooby-Doo character: short, with a chin-length brown bob haircut and bangs, a baggy orange turtleneck sweater, a short red pleated skirt, orange knee socks and red shoes. Appears only once per panel.
- Velma's glasses: she owns ONE pair of thick square black-framed glasses. In panel 1 they lie on the floor and her face is bare. She is NOT in panel 2. She wears them in panels 3 and 4.
- The Mummy: ONE single person, a tall, lumbering cartoon mummy wrapped in loose, dirty-white bandages, with glowing yellow eyes. Secretly a grumpy old museum curator: a real HUMAN with a bald head, a white mustache and a bow tie.
- The Mystery Machine: the classic 1960s van, pale teal-green with an orange stripe and big orange flowers painted on its side.

STORY:
Panel 1: A spooky museum at night in deep blue, with simple shapes of a sarcophagus and an Egyptian statue. Exactly three figures, left to right: the hero, Velma, and the Mummy. In the middle, Velma, fully visible, crawls on her hands and knees toward the viewer, one hand reaching out and patting the floor. Her face is turned toward us and is BARE: her eyes are squeezed tightly shut into two curved lines, eyebrows scrunched, freckles showing, mouth open in a worried wince. Her glasses lie on the floor in front of her hands, just out of reach. On the right, the Mummy looms just behind her with its arms out, and she has no idea. On the left, the hero peeks in from the left edge of the panel, wide-eyed. Velma appears only once in this panel. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to Velma's mouth: "MY GLASSES! I CAN'T SEE!"
Panel 2: Only the hero and the Mummy are in this panel, against a flat purple background with jagged action lines. The hero dives heroically across the panel and snatches the glasses off the floor, just as the Mummy's bandaged hands grab at empty air. Sound effect "WHOOSH!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, appearing only once, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 3: The museum floor in grass green and brown. Exactly three people, left to right: the hero, the curator, and Velma. On the left, the hero holds the loose end of a long bandage, having unraveled the Mummy, revealing the grumpy old curator in the middle, standing in a pile of bandages, arms crossed. Velma MUST be in this panel, standing on the right in the foreground, now wearing her glasses again, pointing at him triumphantly. Velma appears only once in this panel. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to Velma's mouth: "JINKIES! IT WAS THE CURATOR!"
Panel 4: A sunrise sky in flat horizontal bands of orange and lemon yellow, with a big half-circle sun over simple rolling hills. The hero and Velma, wearing her glasses, sit side by side on the roof of the Mystery Machine, sharing burgers, fries and two milkshakes from a plain brown paper takeout bag with nothing written on it, smiling and watching the sunrise. Velma leans toward the hero. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to Velma's mouth: "SAME TIME NEXT MYSTERY?"

TEXT: Exactly 3 speech bubbles (panels 1, 3 and 4) and 1 sound effect (panel 2). Keep every line exactly as written; they are short on purpose. No writing on the takeout bag. The van may show its "THE MYSTERY MACHINE" logo.
