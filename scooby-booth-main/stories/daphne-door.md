# Daphne: Don't Open That Door

CHARACTERS:
- Daphne: the classic Scooby-Doo character: slim, with long, bright orange-red hair in a flip, a purple headband, a short purple dress with lighter lavender trim, a lime green scarf tied at the neck, pink tights and purple shoes. Curious, cheerful, completely fearless. Same look in every panel. Appears only once per panel.
- The Scary Door: a tall, ancient wooden door at the end of a mansion hallway, obviously the scariest door ever: dripping green slime, heavy rusty chains hanging across it, deep claw marks gouged into the wood, thick cobwebs, a skull-shaped door knocker, and eerie green light glowing from the gap underneath. It is the ONLY door in the comic: draw this exact same door, with the same slime, chains, claw marks, skull knocker and green glow, in every panel.
- The Monster: ONE single hulking, shaggy purple monster with huge fangs, horns and glowing yellow eyes. It is a real monster, not a costume. It appears ONLY in panel 2. There is only one monster in the whole comic.

STORY:
Panel 1: A dim mansion hallway at night in deep blue and purple, with the Scary Door at the end. Exactly two people, left to right: Daphne and the hero. On the left, Daphne cheerfully reaches for the skull-shaped knocker, smiling with curiosity. On the right, the hero stands still with both palms held up in a "stop!" gesture, eyes wide, trying to stop her. Draw the hero only once, in one single pose (no motion copies or repeated figures). ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to Daphne's mouth: "OOH! WHAT'S BEHIND THIS DOOR?"
Panel 2: Flat lime green background with jagged action lines. Exactly three figures, left to right: Daphne, the hero, and the Monster. The Scary Door has swung wide open on the right, and the Monster fills the whole doorway, roaring with its mouth wide open and claws raised. Daphne and the hero are frozen stiff in shock, hair blown back by the roar. Daphne appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "ROAAAR!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 3: The same dim mansion hallway at night in deep blue and purple as panel 1, with the walls and floor visible. Exactly two people, left to right: Daphne and the hero. The same Scary Door from panels 1 and 2 (dripping green slime, rusty chains, claw marks, skull-shaped knocker, green glow underneath) is slammed shut again, rattling with shake lines. Daphne calmly holds the door shut with one hand, looking unbothered. The hero leans back against the door with their whole body, pressing it shut, panting and sweating. No monster visible. Daphne appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "SLAM!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 4: The hallway in deep blue, with the same Scary Door from panels 1 to 3 (dripping green slime, rusty chains, claw marks, skull-shaped knocker, green glow underneath), now closed, a few wisps of smoke curling from underneath. Exactly two people, left to right: the hero and Daphne. On the right, Daphne reaches for the skull-shaped knocker AGAIN, smiling brightly and hopefully. On the left, the hero grabs Daphne's arm with both hands, pulling her back, with a horrified, exhausted face. Daphne appears only once in this panel. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing clearly to Daphne's mouth: "MAYBE IT'S FRIENDLIER NOW?"

TEXT: Exactly 2 speech bubbles (panels 1 and 4) and 2 sound effects (panels 2 and 3). Keep every line exactly as written; they are short on purpose. No writing on the door, the walls or any signs.
