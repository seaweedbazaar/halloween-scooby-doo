# Daphne: The Mannequin Monster

CHARACTERS:
- Daphne: the classic Scooby-Doo character: slim, with long, bright orange-red hair in a flip, a purple headband, a short purple dress with lighter lavender trim, a lime green scarf tied at the neck, pink tights and purple shoes. Fashionable, dramatic and expressive. Appears only once per panel.
- The Mannequin Monster: ONE single person, a grumpy old lady bargain hunter disguised as a mannequin. She wears a smooth, blank white plastic mannequin mask with glowing red eyes, white gloves, and a plain, clean mustard-yellow 1970s pantsuit, the same in every panel. There is no separate mannequin body; the monster is just her in the mask.

STORY:
Panel 1: A groovy 1970s department store, dimly lit in purple and deep blue, with racks of colorful clothes and a few plain mannequins on display stands. Exactly three figures, left to right: Daphne, the hero, and the Mannequin Monster. On the left, Daphne excitedly holds up a bright patterned outfit against the hero to see how it looks. In the middle, the hero smiles. In the background on the right, standing among the mannequins, the Mannequin Monster in her mustard-yellow pantsuit has turned her head to stare at them with glowing red eyes, and they haven't noticed. Daphne appears only once in this panel. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to Daphne's mouth: "THE BIG SALE STARTS TOMORROW... BUT I GOT US IN EARLY!"
Panel 2: Flat orange background with jagged action lines. Exactly three figures, left to right: the Mannequin Monster, Daphne, and the hero. On the left, the Mannequin Monster lunges at Daphne with stiff, outstretched white-gloved hands. In the middle, Daphne leans back in a dramatic swoon of terror, one hand on her forehead. On the right, the hero, behind her, looks shocked. Daphne appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "EEEEK!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 3: The store floor in grass green and brown. Exactly three people, left to right: the unmasked old lady, the hero, and Daphne. The hero has shoved a heavy rolling clothing rack into the Mannequin Monster, knocking her down. On the left, she sits on the floor, tangled in clothes and hangers, holding her white mannequin mask in one gloved hand, having just pulled it off. Her real face is revealed: a grumpy old lady with gray hair in pink curlers, still in her mustard-yellow pantsuit. She appears exactly once, with exactly one head. In the middle, the hero stands tall and heroic. On the right, Daphne clasps her hands, looking at the hero, amazed and smitten. Daphne appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "CRASH!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 4: A bright, sunny city street outside the store, with a flat sky blue sky and a few simple white clouds. The hero and Daphne walk out the front doors arm in arm, both loaded down with lots of colorful shopping bags and boxes, grinning. Daphne leans happily against the hero's shoulder, and a few small pink hearts float above her head. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing clearly to Daphne's mouth: "JEEPERS! WE GOT FIRST PICK OF THE WHOLE SALE!"

TEXT: Exactly 2 speech bubbles (panels 1 and 4) and 2 sound effects (panels 2 and 3). No store names, no price tags, no writing on the shopping bags or boxes.
