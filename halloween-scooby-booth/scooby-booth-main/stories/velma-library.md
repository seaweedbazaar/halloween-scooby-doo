# Velma: The Shushing Ghost

CHARACTERS:
- Velma: the classic Scooby-Doo character: short, with a chin-length brown bob haircut and bangs, thick square black-framed glasses, a baggy orange turtleneck sweater, a short red pleated skirt, orange knee socks and red shoes. Same look in every panel. Appears only once per panel.
- The Ghost Librarian: ONE single person, a glowing pale blue ghost draped in a long white sheet, with hollow black eyes. Secretly the grumpy old librarian: under the sheet she is a real HUMAN with gray hair in a bun, cat-eye glasses and a cardigan. She does NOT appear in panels 1 or 2.

STORY:
Panel 1: A dusty old library at night in deep blue and purple, with towering bookshelves and a single desk lamp. Only Velma and the hero are here; there is no ghost anywhere in this panel. Velma crouches by a shelf holding an open old book in one hand, whispering to the hero with one finger of her other hand to her lips. The hero crouches beside her, holding a very dusty old book and shining a flashlight on Velma's page. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to Velma's mouth: "THE GHOST ONLY SHOWS UP WHEN SOMEONE MAKES NOISE..."
Panel 2: The library in flat orange with radiating blast lines. A puff of dust from the old book made the hero sneeze: the hero lets out one enormous sneeze, head thrown forward, eyes squeezed shut. Velma, beside the hero, flinches with both hands over her ears, horrified. Still no ghost. Sound effect "AH-CHOO!" in big hand-drawn letters in the background, NOT in a speech bubble. No speech bubbles in this panel.
Panel 3: The library in eerie flat lime green. Exactly three figures, left to right: the Ghost Librarian, the hero, and Velma. On the left, the Ghost Librarian swoops in from between the shelves, glowing, one long finger pressed to where her lips would be. In the middle, the hero; on the right, Velma; both stand frozen, startled. Velma appears only once in this panel. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to the Ghost Librarian: "QUIET IN MY LIBRARY!"
Panel 4: The library in deep blue. Exactly three people, left to right: the librarian, the hero, and Velma. On the left, the unmasked librarian, the grumpy old woman with a gray bun, cat-eye glasses and a cardigan, stands with the pulled-off white sheet in a heap on the floor at her feet, glaring at them with one finger pressed firmly to her lips. In the middle, the hero; on the right, Velma. The hero and Velma each cover their mouths with both hands, trying very hard not to laugh. Velma appears only once in this panel, and the hero appears only once. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing clearly to the librarian's mouth: "SHHHHHH!"

TEXT: Exactly 3 speech bubbles (panels 1, 3 and 4) and 1 sound effect (panel 2). No writing on the books, shelves or signs.
