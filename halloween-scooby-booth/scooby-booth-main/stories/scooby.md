# Scooby: The Phantom Chef

CHARACTERS:
- Scooby-Doo: the classic cartoon Great Dane, drawn exactly like Scooby in Image 1: brown with a few black spots, a teal collar with a gold diamond-shaped tag, a long snout, droopy ears. Appears only once per panel.
- The Phantom Chef: ONE single person, a glowing pale green ghost in a tall white chef's hat and white apron, with hollow black eyes and long grabby fingers. Secretly a grumpy, mustached old cook in a rubber ghost mask and costume.

STORY:
Panel 1: A 1950s diner kitchen at night in moonlit purple and deep blue, with a black-and-white checkered floor and moonlight through a window. The hero points to an empty, ransacked shelf with a determined look, explaining the plan. Scooby stands beside the hero, nodding eagerly and licking his lips. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to the hero's mouth: "THE GHOST STEALS FOOD EVERY NIGHT. LET'S BAIT HIM WITH THE BEST SANDWICH EVER!"
Panel 2: The fridge door is wide open, its bright lemon yellow light spilling across the counter, with the kitchen in warm brown and orange around it. The hero and Scooby happily build two towering bait sandwiches on the counter, tossing ham, salami, cheese, tomato slices, lettuce and pickles through the air. The hero has a proud, mischievous grin, like a kid building something awesome. Scooby sneaks a taste of one slice of ham. A fish tail sticks out of one sandwich. Fun and playful, not villainous. No text in this panel.
Panel 3: The classic Hanna-Barbera giant sandwich gag: the bait turned out too good to resist. A flat blue-gray background with streaky horizontal brush strokes, like a 1969 cartoon background. Exactly three figures, left to right: the Phantom Chef, Scooby-Doo, and the hero. Only ONE human hero in this panel; the figure next to the hero is Scooby-Doo, the brown Great Dane dog, NOT a second person. In the middle, Scooby-Doo, the brown spotted Great Dane with his teal collar, stands on his hind legs in profile facing right, holding a towering sandwich in his front paws, his long dog snout and jaws stretched impossibly wide open. On the right, the hero stands in profile facing right, holding their own towering sandwich in both hands at mouth height, mouth stretched wide open, eyes squeezed shut with joy. Each sandwich is a wobbly stack about as tall as a head, with at least ten thick layers: wavy green lettuce, red tomato slices, pink ham, orange cheese, salami, pickles, and bread on top and bottom. On the left, sneaking up behind them, the Phantom Chef reaches one long, grabby green hand toward Scooby's sandwich, and they have no idea. Scooby appears only once in this panel, and the hero appears only once. No text in this panel.
Panel 4: A burst of orange action lines radiating from the center. Exactly three figures, left to right: the cook, Scooby, and the hero. On the left, the unmasked villain sits on the checkered floor, still wearing the white ghost costume, apron and tall chef's hat, now crooked. He holds the pulled-off green ghost mask in one hand, revealing the grumpy, mustached old cook underneath. One floppy rubber ghost arm has torn off his costume. In the middle, Scooby lies on the floor nearby, happily gnawing on that limp green rubber arm like a chew toy, holding it in his paws. On the right, the hero stands beside them, tucking one giant sandwich under one arm and pointing at the cook with the other hand, grinning. Scooby appears only once in this panel. Sound effect "CHOMP!" in big yellow letters in the background. ONE speech bubble, its tail pointing to the hero's mouth: "HE COULDN'T RESIST THAT SANDWICH!"

TEXT: Exactly 2 speech bubbles (panels 1 and 4) and 1 sound effect (panel 4). No writing on the food, the shelves or the fridge.
